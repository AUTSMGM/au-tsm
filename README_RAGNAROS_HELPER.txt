AU TSM Ragnaros Helper

- Uses the generated molten Ragnaros artwork on the left side.
- Ragnaros does not appear initially.
- When the Lich King is dismissed, Ragnaros transitions in from the left.
- His integrated molten speech frame is used for dynamic text.
- Most responses twist classic Ragnaros-style lines into jokes about Roks.
- Clicking Ragnaros or ASK FOR HELP generates another random line.
- Ragnaros can be dismissed independently.
- Both helpers remain above the normal site UI.

No Supabase changes are required.
