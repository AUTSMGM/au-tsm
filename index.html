The Guild

Who we are

AU TSM is an Oceanic Horde guild playing World of Warcraft Forever.


The guild is primarily made up of Australian and New Zealand players and is focused on building a long-term community for levelling, dungeons, progression, raids, professions and everything else Azeroth has to offer.


The goal is simple: build a strong guild, enjoy the game with good people and stick around for the journey.
